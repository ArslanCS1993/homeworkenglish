The task

Write about yourself and one other person — a friend or a family member.

  1. Write 5–8 sentences (about 80–120 words).
  2. Say who you are: your name, your age, where you are from, what you do.
  3. Say something about the other person: who they are and what they are like.
  4. Use all three forms — amisare — at least twice each.

Write the full forms here (I am, not I'm) — short forms don't count toward the target.
